# Third-Party Notices

The website carries two third-party works, both typefaces.

## Inter

Copyright (c) 2016 The Inter Project Authors, <https://github.com/rsms/inter>

Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The full licence is at
[`src/assets/fonts/OFL-Inter.txt`](src/assets/fonts/OFL-Inter.txt), and is
published with the site at `/assets/fonts/OFL-Inter.txt`.

Inter is served to readers who are not on an Apple platform, where the system
stack resolves to SF Pro instead. The files in `src/assets/fonts/` are the
variable upright face, split by script into the subsets the site needs: Latin,
Latin Extended, Vietnamese, Greek, Greek Extended, Cyrillic and Cyrillic
Extended. No file has been modified; they are the subsets published by the
Fontsource project.

## IBM Plex

Copyright © 2017 IBM Corp., with Reserved Font Name "Plex".
<https://github.com/IBM/plex>

Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The full licence is at
[`src/assets/fonts/OFL-IBM-Plex.txt`](src/assets/fonts/OFL-IBM-Plex.txt), and is
published with the site at `/assets/fonts/OFL-IBM-Plex.txt`.

IBM Plex Sans sets the headings, and IBM Plex Mono sets format names, section
numbers and the specification line. Plex Sans is the variable upright face,
subset to Latin, Latin Extended, Vietnamese, Greek, Cyrillic and Cyrillic
Extended; Plex Mono is two static weights of Latin alone, which is all the
format names need. No file has been modified.
